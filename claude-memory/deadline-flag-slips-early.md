For a deliverable with a fixed time (the work project, "zip by 6 am"), the user counts the deadline as met only if the version they think of as final arrives by then.

On Oct 21 a verified v1 was sent at 04:2x. The reviewed v2 came at 07:02 and the final v2.1 at 08:55. At 08:55 the user said "U were supposed to give it at 6 it's 9 lol".

**Why:** a safety copy sent while asleep, with review rounds still running, did not read as "delivered" to them. The slip past 06:00 was never announced before 06:00.

**How to apply:**
- Plan review rounds so the final version lands before the deadline: fewer parallel heavy agents, and the reviews started earlier.
- If it will still slip, tell the user before the deadline, in one line, with the new time and what the on-time version already covers.
- At the deadline, send one clearly labelled "this is the one to use" message.
