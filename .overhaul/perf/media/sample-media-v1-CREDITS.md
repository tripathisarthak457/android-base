# Sample clips

The sample media of android-base's catalog app (its Reels scene). Each clip is in the public domain under
CC0 1.0, its author's own work, trimmed to at most 10 s and transcoded once to H.264 (High, 720x1280,
30 fps, about 1.5 Mbit/s, no audio, faststart) with a JPEG poster of its first frame. Source metadata
(locations, device names) was removed.
