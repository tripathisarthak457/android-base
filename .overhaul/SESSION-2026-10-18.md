## Lessons (Oct 17-18)

- **A hung build blocks everything:** WP06's `check` hung on an app-lock unit test with an idle CPU and held the Gradle lock
  for 2.5 hours (Oct 17). `locked` has no timeout, so the autopilot kills a build that holds the lock over 75 minutes with
  the CPU under 15% for 30 minutes and notes it in the owning handoff. WP06 found and fixed the test.
- **Battery standby:** at 06:46 on Oct 18 the laptop went to battery and Modern Standby suspended it for an hour; the
  autopilot now warns when `Win32_Battery.BatteryStatus` is not 2.
- **The app's process can restart** (Oct 17 18:45, Oct 18 10:41): every cron, Monitor and background task dies with it.
  Workflow runs survive as journals and resume from this session.
- **Resume replays the longest unchanged prefix:** calls started after an interrupted one re-run (WIN's reviews re-ran
  twice). Stop and resume as rarely as possible; script edits take effect only at the next resume.
- **Usage pace:** about 11-15 points per 20 minutes with three workflows. A 20-minute check overshoots a cap, so near a cap
  the autopilot adds 7-minute one-shot checks.
- **Backup guards caught two leaks before they were published:** `out/WP04-perf/s/` (source snapshots; an old commit's
  `web/deploy/install.sh` has a local database password) and raw phone `logcat*.txt` dumps; both are excluded in
  `handover/backup-include.txt`, and stale untracked copies in `wt/_workspace-branch` must be recycled after a guard stops.
- **Memory:** one Gradle build (3 GB daemon + 2 x 2 GB Kotlin daemons) plus agents takes free RAM down to 200-500 MB; the
  page file grows, nothing crashed. One build at a time stays the rule.
