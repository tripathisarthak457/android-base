### Layering contract as built (A3.9)

- Registration: `entry<K>(presentation = Presentation.Sheet(detents) | Presentation.Cover)`; `navigator.navigate(key)`
  and deep links route it to `ShellState`'s presentation stack (a link selects the owning tab first). The stack is flat.
- `PresentationHost` is composed only while the stack is non-empty or an exit runs, above the bar; its `NavDisplay` is
  registered after the tab hosts, so its Back wins. Without a `sheetStrategy`, sheets are shown as covers (WP03 passes
  `SheetScene` through `AppShell(sheetStrategy = …)`).
- Window chrome: only `WindowChromeWriter` in `AppShell` writes `LocalBottomChrome` and `LocalWindowTopChrome`, only
  while nothing moves. Bottom: a presented screen's own bottom bar > keyboard (`imeAnimationTarget`) > the
  screen-level bar of a screen that hides the tab bar > the bar's settled height (0 beside a rail). Top: the front
  screen's top bar (presented first). Both reset to 0 when the shell leaves (sign-out). Every entry gets its own
  `ScreenChrome` (`LocalScreenChrome`): a screen-level bottom bar (`AppBottomActionBar`, WP03) writes `bottomBar`, the
  scaffold writes `topBar` and `background` (WP03), and the primary list reports its scroll edge
  (`ReportScrollEdge`).
- `LocalTabBarVisible` is true inside tab hosts on compact widths, false on screens that hide the bar, in presented
  screens and outside the shell. WP03's debug assertion in `AppBottomActionBar` reads it.
- The bar hides under the keyboard in the layout phase (`hideUnderKeyboard()`: translated by the IME inset,
  `alpha = 0` once covered). Fully covered by a screen that hides it, the bar stays composed but is not placed and is
  cleared from semantics (no draw, no touch, nothing read out, nothing to recompose on the pop).
- Not built here: layers 4–5 (`AppOverlayHost`, toasts) are WP03's, against `LocalBottomChrome`.

### A3.4a behaviours and mechanisms

| Situation | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Double push of one key | `guard()`: a `Navigate` with `singleTop` whose key is the top → `Drop` (always). |
| Two pushes within one transition | `guard()`: a `Navigate` while the host's gate is moving and its last change was a push → `Drop`. Any other command while moving → `Wait` (the shell's collector suspends until `moving` is false, then submits again). |
| Tab tap during motion | The incoming tab is revealed (12 dp nudge + 0.6 veil on `thumb`, incoming tab only); the leaving tab is parked at once (placed, layer at alpha 0 off the window, semantics cleared) and its slide runs to its end there, so it never shows a half frame when it comes back; its gate keeps counting its own presses. |
| Reselect | `ShellState.select(i)` on the selected tab: one pop of the whole stack through the gate (one animated multi-pop); at the root → `reselections[i]++` → `ScrollToTopOnReselect`; while moving → ignored. |
| Back during the identity crossfade | `EntryHost` registers a consuming `NavigationBackHandler` while the `updateTransition` runs. Sheet dismiss idempotency is WP03's. |
| Fold, unfold, resize | `StackPlan` gives `Slide.None` when the stack is the same and only the pane layout changed (`StackPlanTest`, `PaneTest`, `ResizeContinuityTest`); a same-class resize composes no screen (`ShellRecompositionTest`). |
| Process death | `rememberShellState` saver: `[selected root, presented, stack…]`, each stack matched to its tab by root key; only the selected tab composes on restore (`RestoreTest`, `restore` scenario). |
| Rapid Back | `BackGate`: counted while moving (including the gap between a stack change and its first animation frame), popped together once the transition is at 99 % of its travel or settled, never past the floor (`RapidBackTest`). |
| Back gesture | Over a settled stack, or over a push still sliding in (REQ 32), the back card starts from where the screens are; the page beneath springs to rest under it. The commit runs inside `gate.hold`, pops with `StackChange.GesturePop` (no slide), and Back pressed meanwhile is counted. A swipe during a pop, `EDGE_NONE` progress (3-button bar, Back key) and API < 34 never start a card: they pop with the slide or are counted (`BackCardTest`). While the card is opaque, the page beneath and its scrim are drawn only around it, in up to four whole-pixel scissor strips with the card's corners left to them (`StackScene.drawAroundCard`, `55e144d` from `wp/WP04-perf`; `BackCardDrawingTest` checks every pixel around and under the card). |

## Deviations from MASTER-PLAN, with evidence

1. **BackGate threshold**: `SETTLED_ENOUGH = 0.99` of the travel (distance), not 0.85 of the duration. Compose exposes
   no play time for a spring-driven `Transition`; the arrival fraction is the spring's progress, and 99 % of travel is
   about 70 % of the settle time (0.24 s response: 254 ms of 356 ms). The effect is the plan's: the coalesced pop
   starts when the arriving screen is within a few pixels.
2. **NavGuard during a pop**: a push arriving during a *pop* waits for it to settle instead of being dropped (only a push
   during a push is dropped). Commands from effects and user taps both arrive through `AppNavigator`, so the shell
   cannot tell a tap from an effect; dropping every push mid-motion would silently drop an effect's navigation (a
   screen that redirects as it opens).
3. **Predictive pop completion** (step 12 question): Navigation 3 1.2.0 cannot finish a released gesture on a spring.
   `NavDisplay` animates the remaining fraction itself with `tween(remainingDuration)` (default FastOutSlowIn) through
   `SeekableTransitionState.seekTo` (bytecode of `NavDisplayKt__NavDisplayKt$NavDisplay$11$1` calls
   `AnimationSpecKt.tween$default` and `SuspendAnimationKt.animate$default`; the same code is lines 754–790 of the
   1.1.7 `NavDisplay.kt` source), with `remainingDuration = (1 − fraction) × totalDuration` of the predictive spec.
   With `predictivePop`'s linear track lasting the spring's settle time, a released swipe finishes at a Back press's
   pace. Using the fling velocity would need a fork of `NavDisplay`; not done.
4. **Warm-pop spike (A3.10)**: option A (`KeepUnderScene`) adopted from the source finding and `KeepUnderTest`, before
   phone numbers (phone locked). Navigation 3 1.2.0 still wraps every entry in `SceneSetupNavEntryDecorator`, a
   `movableContentOf` per content key (`SceneSetupNavEntryDecorator.class` calls `MovableContentKt.movableContentOf`;
   `SceneStateKt.rememberSceneState` applies it with `rememberBackStackAwareLifecycleNavEntryDecorator`), so the entry
   under the top, composed inside `KeepUnderScene`, moves into the next scene on a pop instead of being composed
   again. `KeepUnderTest` asserts the same composition survives the pop. The emulator shows the pop's first frame
   halved (Measurements). Superseded by S1 (`StackScene`, option B: one parent, parked pages to depth 3); on the
   phone a pop's first frame is now 4.5–5.1 / 11.0 ms median / max (`$P/phase3c`, `$P/phase3d`) against 8 / 16.
5. **Presentation without SheetScene**: until WP03 passes `SheetScene`, sheet keys present as covers.
6. **Hidden tabs are parked, not left unmeasured** (S4, run 5): measured and placed with their layer at alpha 0 off
   the window, so a switch changes two layer properties. The plan's unmeasured child dropped every display list.
   The phone runs `tabs`, `nav_stress` and `restore` with no crash.
7. **Touch-for-compile edits** in `CatalogComponents.kt`/`CatalogSection.kt` went into feature commits in run 1 rather
   than separate "Update call sites" commits.

## Rules and gotchas the docs must state

Navigation and the shell (for `AGENTS.md`, `docs/NAVIGATION.md`, `MOTION-AND-PERFORMANCE.md`):

- Features never touch a back stack or `NavDisplay`: they register `entry<Key>(pane = …, presentation = …)` in their
  nav module and navigate through `AppNavigator`. Sheets, covers and action sheets are registered with
  `presentation = Presentation.Sheet(…)`/`Cover`, never presented inside a tab.
- A key another feature opens lives in the owner's `api/` package (or the shared-key folder); only the owner registers
  it (`SharedDestinationsTest`). Never key composition on `ShellTab` (its `ImageVector` hashes by identity); a tab's
  identity is its root key.
- Every scrolling container in a tab, root or pushed, pads with `chromeContentPadding(base)`; the bar floats over
  content at every depth and the bottom edge fade sits between them. A root screen passes its primary list's state to
  `ScrollToTopOnReselect`. Never pad a root above the bar (requirement 17's opaque block).
- `chromeContentPadding()` reads the chrome when the list measures (as inset padding does), and its bottom is never
  less than the navigation bar: never read `LocalBottomChrome.current.value` in composition to pad a list, which
  recomposes the screen on every chrome change and leaves the gesture handle uncovered beside a rail.
- Every screen reports its primary scrolling container with `ReportScrollEdge(state)` next to the container (hoist
  `rememberScrollState()` for a scrolling column). A docked bar shows its surface and rule, and the bottom edge fade
  its gradient, only while content runs under them (iOS's scroll edge appearance), so a list resting on its end is
  never cut by a hard edge or veiled; a screen that reports nothing keeps the surface.
- The app's theme never recreates the activity: MainActivity handles `uiMode` and sets the app's night mode from the
  theme setting, so the next cold start's splash is already in the app's theme. Anything that reads night-mode
  resources must read them through Compose (`LocalConfiguration`), not once at inflation.
- Tab labels grow with the font scale up to 1.3× and a label too long for its slot is set smaller (to 0.85×) before
  it is ellipsized; a docked bar keeps 8 dp between its outer tabs and the screen edges.
- A destination that must hide the bar implements `FullScreenKey` (or the app sets `ShellConfig(hideBarOnPush = true)`).
- Screens draw an opaque background: several entries are composed and placed at once (`EntryFrame` paints
  `colors.background`).
- **A screen's first composition is final.** Load secondary sections (related rows, a second list) after the slide:
  `LocalNavigationSettled.current` is a `() -> Boolean` to read in an effect (`snapshotFlow { settled() }.first { it }`),
  never in composition. Seed a detail from what the caller or a cache already has (WP02-M3, WP06 `peek`).
- Onboarding and sign-in completion never navigate: the saved flag or session swaps the host.
- Programmatic navigation during a transition waits; a second push during a push is dropped; Back pressed while the
  screens move is counted and applied as one pop at 99 % of travel, never past the root.
- Tab switches never crossfade: the incoming tab is nudged 12 dp on `thumb`, with nothing drawn over it (no veil:
  a full-screen rectangle on the switch's first frames, while the GPU is clocked down, cost a janky frame per
  switch); the outgoing tab is parked at once. A deep link, a navigation to a root and reduce motion switch with no
  motion.
- During a slide only the strip of the lower page the upper page has not reached is drawn (a scissor clip); the
  covered part is never drawn and painted over. Transitions start while the GPU is still clocked down from idle, so
  their first frames are where fill costs a frame.
- The window has no visible background once the app has drawn (its `ColorDrawable` is transparent). Every surface
  must be opaque or lie over an opaque one; anything that fades whole screens paints its own backdrop while it runs
  (`EntryHost` does). Never add a full-screen background under a screen that already paints one.
- A key may be in a stack more than once (a profile opened from the profile it led to, a row selected again beside
  its list): each is its own screen with its own state and ViewModel, its content key numbered by the entries of that
  key below it. Shell code keys per-screen state by content key, never by `AppNavKey`.
- Hidden tabs and covered pages are **parked** (placed, layer at alpha 0 off the window, semantics cleared), never
  unplaced or moved to another parent; a hidden tab is capped at CREATED and its back dispatcher is off; the page under
  the top is STARTED, deeper pages CREATED; pages deeper than three leave composition and come back from saved state.
- The predictive back is Gmail's card (SPEC): scale to 0.90, 8 dp far-edge gap, follows the finger up to 24 dp,
  28 dp corners, 0.32 scrim in 50 ms; the commit returns to full size while fading for 100 ms and pops with no slide;
  cancel follows the system's spring back in about 100 ms. It runs from any moment of a push (requirement 32); a
  swipe during a pop is counted. Its progress is read in layer and draw blocks only. `EDGE_NONE`, the Back key and
  API < 34 pop with the slide. At the start tab's root no handler is enabled, so the system's back-to-home runs.
- Every full-screen move rides one spring, `screen` = response 0.344 s, no bounce; the covered page moves a third as
  far (parallax 0.333) under a drawn scrim (0.10 light, 0.24 dark) and a 0.04 edge shadow. No alpha or scale on whole
  screens except the back card's 100 ms `ModulateAlpha` commit fade.
- The slide's clock starts on the frame after the one that composes the new screen, so the heavy frame shows nothing
  moving (SMOOTHNESS S3.1).
- Bar: labels `Always` by default; the indicator moves on two edges (lead 0.22 s, trail 0.32 s, stretch ≤ 1.5 slots,
  squash 0.06); the icon dips to 0.84 and springs back; the pill is 44 dp in the 64 dp capsule and a label never
  passes it (ellipsized, text capped at 1.3×). `BarMinimize.OnScrollDown` folds after 10 dp of scroll in one direction
  on `thumb`, and opens on a scroll up, at the top or on a tap; it is never scrubbed by the scroll.
- Navigation is per window: `AppNavigator`, `PendingDeepLink`, `NavRegistry` and `NavKeySerialization` are
  `@ActivityRetainedScoped`, and every feature's nav module installs in `ActivityRetainedComponent`.
- Adaptive chrome: bar on compact widths, rail on medium/expanded and in short windows, sidebar from Large width;
  more than 5 tabs on a bar (7 on a rail) list the rest under More. Panes come from the registration
  (`NavPane.List/Detail/Extra/Main/Supporting`); a screen reads `LocalPaneContext` (role, `beside`, `selectedKey`) and
  hides its back arrow when shown beside its list. A fold or resize never slides and composes nothing within a class.
- The activity is never recreated by a resize, fold, keyboard or orientation change (`configChanges` in the
  manifest); never lock orientation or resizability.
- Keyboard: the shell answers Ctrl+1…9, Ctrl+[ and Alt+←, Ctrl+Shift+S; a screen adds its own with
  `ShortcutEffect(Shortcut(Key.F, label, ctrl = true)) { … }`, which answers only while it is resumed.
  `MainActivity.dispatchKeyEvent` offers keys to the shortcuts before the focused view; lint flags any override of
  it on a `ComponentActivity` as `RestrictedApi` (androidx.core's restricted base class overrides it), so the
  override carries a scoped `@SuppressLint` with that reason. Never move shortcuts to `onKeyShortcut`, which a text
  field with focus can pre-empt.
- Each kept tab gets its own `NavigationEventDispatcher`, enabled in the switching composition (not a frame later, as
  `rememberNavigationEventDispatcherOwner` does).
- A Back handler whose enabled state follows navigation state is a `NavigationEventHandler` registered with
  `BackHandlerEffect(handler) { enabled }`, not `NavigationBackHandler(isBackEnabled = …)`: the parameter recomposes
  the caller every time it flips, and the stack's flips in the middle of a pop.
- Never pass state into a `SubcomposeLayout`'s children through `rememberUpdatedState`: the parent writes it while it
  recomposes, so the children recompose a frame late. Give them the state the event writes (a `() -> T` that reads
  it), and they recompose in the event's frame.
- A screen is filled once per frame. The entry's frame paints the background and says so (`LocalPaintedBackground`);
  `AppScaffold`'s default background is `Unspecified` on such a screen (`AppScaffoldDefaults.background()`) and a
  scaffold paints only a colour of its own. A full-window fill is 2.6 Mpx, about a third of a frame's GPU time on
  the Redmi, spent in the first frame of a transition where the GPU is still clocked down.
- A leaving screen keeps every static local it was composed with until it has slid off: `LocalPaneContext` is kept
  for a popped entry (`rememberPaneContexts`), because a new value for a static local composes the whole subtree in
  the pop's first frame (416 composables of the sample detail).
- A handler on a bar cell reads the selection through state (`rememberUpdatedState`), never captures it: a
  captured index is a new lambda per switch, and every cell's `selectable` recomposes to take it.
- A shape clipped or filled on every frame of a motion is a rounded rect (`addRoundRect`), never a path of cubics:
  Skia clips and fills a rounded rect analytically and masks a general path per frame (the pill's two cell layers).

Measuring (for `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`):

- Release builds only (`devBenchmarkRelease` with the benchmark module, else `devRelease`), compiled with their
  profile, on a real phone. `perf.sh` refuses a locked phone and measures taps from the finger's lift.
- `perf.sh --gate solid` is the gate; `record.sh` makes frame strips (always `--size 720x1600` on the Redmi).
